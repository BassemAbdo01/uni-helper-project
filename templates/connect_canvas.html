Get your access token

  1. In Canvas, open Account → Settings.
  2. Scroll to Approved Integrations and press + New Access Token. Put Nexora as the purpose and leave the expiry blank.
  3. Press Generate Token and copy it. Canvas shows it once — if you close that box you will need a new one.
