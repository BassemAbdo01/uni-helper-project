Keep this calendar in Google Calendar

Google subscribes to your private Nexora calendar, so every deadline, midterm and exam here shows up there — and each time Nexora syncs with your LMS, Google picks up the changes on its own.

Add to Google Calendar

Google opens with your calendar filled in; press Add there and you are done. If you are signed in to more than one Google account, it uses the one that is open.

Apple Calendar / Outlook

Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule, up to once a day, so a deadline synced tonight may only appear there tomorrow — Nexora itself is always current.
