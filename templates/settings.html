Google Calendar

Subscribe to your deadlines

Nexora gives you a private calendar address. The quickest way to use it is the Sync to Google Calendar button on the calendar; this is where you reset or turn the address off. It works in Apple Calendar and Outlook the same way.

  1. Copy the address above.
  2. In Google Calendar, next to Other calendars, click + → From URL.
  3. Paste it and click Add calendar.
Treat this address like a password: anyone who has it can read your deadlines. Reset link makes a new one and breaks the old, which is the only way to un-share it once it is out. Google decides how often to check for changes, usually every few hours, so a deadline you add here may take a while to appear there.
