{# A student who does not know which one they use cannot be left here. The address bar answers it in one look, and it is the only answer that needs no knowledge of what an LMS is. #}

Not sure which one? Open the website where you submit assignments and look at the address bar. If it contains brightspace or d2l, pick Brightspace. If it contains instructure or canvas, pick Canvas.

You can do this later from Brightspace or Canvas in the sidebar — but Nexora has nothing to show you until one of them is connected.
