# Nexora Grading Accuracy v3.6

This release replaces the single-pass course-outline prompt with a three-pass grading-policy compiler:

1. Forensic fact extraction with evidence quotes.
2. Executable grading-policy compilation.
3. Independent adversarial audit and correction.

A deterministic validator then checks scheme totals, component references, and rule consistency. Low-confidence or contradictory analyses are surfaced to the student and require explicit acknowledgement before confirmation.

This materially reduces silent omissions and hallucinated grading rules, but no AI system can guarantee perfect interpretation of every outline. Nexora continues to require user confirmation.
